Sync Fixtures by Date

Sync fixtures for any specific date, past or future — not just "today and tomorrow." Useful for catching up after downtime or pre-loading a date range ahead of time.

Sync Fixtures Ahead in Bulk

Pulls fixtures one day at a time starting today, out to however many days ahead you set — e.g. 60 for two months — then automatically scores everything it just synced. One API call per day to sync, no API calls to score (scoring is local). Note: scores this far out reflect today's team form, not form on match day, so treat far-future scores as a first pass rather than final.
